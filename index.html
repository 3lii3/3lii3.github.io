About me

My obsession with computers started the moment I got my hands on one, and been passionate about exploring the field ever since!

I was introduced to Scratch 2.0 back in 2017 making simple animations. However, after the 2019 pandemic, I started doing Arduino projects by myself, where I've created basic projects to be familiar with electronics and code. As I enjoyed the field, I decide to join a local Robotics club where I started to learn more day by day.
In late 2023, I began developing Minecraft Bukkit plugins in Java, a framework I still find incredibly fun and interesting.
Early 2025, my interests expanded into Game Design using Roblox Studio and Godot, alongside 3D modeling in Blender.
Today, I'm focusing on Cloud Computing, specially hosting server instances to gain a wider perspective on how our everyday software run.
